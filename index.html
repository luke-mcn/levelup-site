How it works

Four moments a week keep everything moving

No time slots and no rigid schedule. A realistic plan, adjusted to how the week is actually going.

Once a week

Plan the week

Level Up drafts the week: areas spread across the days, exercise mapped out, your focus time protected. Tap a day to change it, then keep it.

Each morning · 2 minutes

Check in

Say how your energy is. See what needs attention. Today's plan resizes to fit, with extras if you've got more in you.

Through the day

Focus and log

Start a timer on anything. It chimes at your estimate. Log what you did and where it lives, and collect the XP.

Each evening

Close the day

See your XP and focus time. Send anything unfinished back to its list, to tomorrow, or to the weekend.

Whatever you move to tomorrow is waiting in tomorrow's check-in, so the loop starts again.

It suggests what's next, and tells you why

Everything in it

Built for steady progress, not busywork

Focus timer

Start from anything. It chimes at your estimate, extends by 15 or 30 minutes, and logs the real time when you finish. Survives a locked phone.

Habits and streaks

Up by 6, lights out by 10, screen time under two hours. Your habits, your goals, with a streak for every day you keep them.

Freezes, not guilt

Start with five. Earn more by levelling up and keeping perfect weeks. One bad day doesn't wipe out a month of work.

A reward shop you stock

Earn XP for every minute of real work. Spend it on rewards you choose and price yourself, with an estimate of how many days each will take.

Count what matters

Books read, km run, conditions covered, hours revised. Make a counter for anything and watch it build across the year.

Capture in seconds

Tasks, topics and questions from anywhere with one button. Questions go to an inbox that nudges you after a day.
